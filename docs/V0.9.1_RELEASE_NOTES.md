# LinkBeacon v0.9.1

This maintenance update improves access to LinkBeacon's privacy policy and
clarifies its existing data-handling summaries. It adds no network-detection
capability.

## Privacy-policy access

- Open the published full privacy policy from the app's Privacy & Data page.
- Open the English or Simplified Chinese policy matching the app language.
- Read clearer summaries of local storage, functional network requests,
  Wi-Fi/location-related access, and deliberate exports/sharing.
- Receive helpful feedback if a browser cannot open the policy; bundled
  summaries remain readable offline.
- Copy the policy link with explicit success or failure feedback.

No network-tool capability, permission, SDK, database migration or data
collection is added in this maintenance release.

## Privacy policy

- [English privacy policy](https://zhibo7841-hue.github.io/LinkBeacon/privacy/)
- [Simplified Chinese privacy policy](https://zhibo7841-hue.github.io/LinkBeacon/privacy/zh-CN/)

The policy is maintained according to actual data practices, not app version
numbers. Opening the full policy requires internet access; the app's concise
summaries and policy-link copying remain available offline.

## Requirements and compatibility

Android 12 / API 31 or later. Application ID remains `com.networktoolbox`.
Existing v0.9.0 installations can upgrade in place with the same application
identity and signing certificate, preserving local History and saved-device
data. Android 12 representative regression passed. This version's Android 16
real-device compatibility spot check has not yet been completed.

This GitHub maintenance release does not mean that LinkBeacon is available on
Google Play or that its Data Safety declaration has been approved. Google Play
signing enrollment and submission remain separate pending steps.
