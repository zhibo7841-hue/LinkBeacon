# LinkBeacon v0.7.1

LinkBeacon v0.7.1 polishes local device profiles and adds a bounded TCP Port
Scan for focused network troubleshooting.

## Highlights

- Improved device profile editing.
- TCP Port Scan from Tools and Device Detail.
- Quick and custom-range scans.
- Open-port results with common-service hints.
- Safer cancellation and network-change handling.

## Device Center

Custom Name, Device Type, and Notes can now be edited together on one dedicated
device-profile page. Changes are validated and saved atomically, while Favorite
and Wake-on-LAN remain independent settings. Device Detail keeps a concise,
read-only local-profile summary and protects unsaved edits on Back.

## Port Scan

Port Scan performs ordinary TCP Connect checks against one IPv4 target or a
hostname that resolves to IPv4. It is available as a standalone tool and from
Device Detail.

- Quick Scan checks an audited set of 24 common TCP ports.
- Custom Range accepts one inclusive range from 1 to 65535.
- Real progress, Stop, partial results, and network-change termination are
  supported.
- Results retain confirmed open ports and aggregate closed/no-response outcomes.
- Port-number labels are common-service hints only; they do not identify the
  running service, software, device type, or security state.

## Reliability

- Scans use a bounded worker pool rather than one unrestricted task per port.
- Cancelling a scan closes active sockets and stops new work.
- A material network change terminates the scan instead of mixing results from
  different networks.
- Large custom ranges require explicit confirmation.

## Compatibility and Privacy

- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Existing v0.7.0 installations can upgrade in place without clearing local data.
- Room remains version 5; no database migration is added in v0.7.1.
- Existing Favorites, Custom Names, Device Types, Notes, Wake-on-LAN settings,
  observation metadata, and History remain compatible.
- Port Scan results stay on-device and are not added to History or Diagnostic
  Reports in this release.

## Current Limitations

- TCP Connect scanning only; no UDP or raw SYN scan.
- IPv4 targets only in Port Scan Phase 1.
- Common-service hints are not service fingerprinting or banner detection.
- No Port Scan History, Report integration, or background scanning.
- LinkBeacon is a network-diagnostics aid, not a vulnerability scanner or Nmap
  replacement.
