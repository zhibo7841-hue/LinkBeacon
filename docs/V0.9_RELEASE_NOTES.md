# LinkBeacon v0.9.0

Release notes draft for the signed RC1. Publication remains pending representative-device acceptance.

LinkBeacon v0.9.0 adds a local, user-initiated Wi-Fi Analyzer alongside the existing network tools.

## Highlights

- Current Wi-Fi connection details and nearby access-point observations.
- Signal strength in dBm, band/channel, security, and available radio details.
- A basic channel overview with observed AP counts and strongest observed signal.
- English and Simplified-Chinese presentation.

## Wi-Fi Analyzer

The current-connection view shows Android-provided SSID/BSSID, signal, frequency,
band, channel, link speed, Wi-Fi standard, and security information when those
fields are available. Missing or redacted fields remain unavailable rather than
being guessed. Home may show the connected SSID when Android provides it.

Nearby Networks lists separate AP observations, including distinct BSSIDs
under one SSID and hidden networks. Search by SSID/BSSID and filter by band.
Manual Refresh requests a new scan; the screen labels fresh, cached, or
unknown-freshness results. A failed or throttled request does not prove that
there are no networks and does not silently turn cached results into fresh ones.

Channels summarizes the *observed* AP count and strongest observed RSSI for
each channel in the 2.4, 5, and 6 GHz bands, with a connected-channel marker
when supported by actual connection evidence. The observations are Android
nearby scan results, not real-time RF spectrum, airtime utilization, or an
interference measurement. No best-channel recommendation or score is provided.
6 GHz networks are shown only when the device, platform, and environment
actually supply observations; a missing observation is not proof of absence.

## Permissions and privacy

Android requires precise location permission and enabled system Location
Services to expose nearby Wi-Fi scan results through the APIs used here. On
Android 12 the app requests Fine and Coarse together so the system can offer
its precise/approximate choice; approximate-only access cannot unlock these
scans. Wi-Fi observations, including SSIDs and BSSIDs, are processed locally
and are not uploaded, saved to History, or included in reports. LinkBeacon
does not continuously track location or scan in the background. Existing
user-initiated network tests still generate their ordinary network traffic.

## Compatibility

- Requires Android 12 or later (`minSdk 31`); targets Android API 36.
- Existing installations retain the `com.networktoolbox` application ID.
- Room remains version 5 with no v0.9 schema change or migration.
- Existing History, saved devices, Favorites, Device Profiles, Wake-on-LAN
  configuration, and language settings remain on their established local paths.
- Android 12 full development regression and Android 16 development
  compatibility regression passed. Exact signed-RC1 representative regression
  is still pending and is not claimed here.

## Known limitations

- Android controls scan availability and throttling; available results may be cached.
- 6 GHz visibility depends on the device, platform, and nearby environment.
- No best-channel recommendation or RF interference measurement.
- No full channel graph, Wi-Fi History, Site Survey, Report/PDF, AP favorite,
  or Automatic Diagnosis integration in this release.
