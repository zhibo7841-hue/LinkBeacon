## Permissions and Location Services

The v0.9 manifest adds `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, paired
`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, and `CHANGE_WIFI_STATE` to the pre-existing network
permissions. The final merged APK permission list is recorded under Automated
and Packaging Gates. No `NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES` permission or `neverForLocation`
assertion is present. The selected nearby-scan APIs require Fine Location and
enabled system Location Services; coarse-only grants do not unlock scans.
Android 12 requests Fine and Coarse together so the platform offers its
precise/approximate choice. Bilingual rationale states Android's scan-access
requirement and local processing; it does not claim that location-sensitive
permission is never accessed or that users are continuously tracked.

## Wi-Fi Evidence and Interpretation Audit

- **Current connection:** available SSID, BSSID, RSSI, band, frequency,
  channel, link speed, standard, and security derive from platform facts;
  unavailable/redacted values are not fabricated. Home shows SSID only when
  the shared current-network context can safely provide it, otherwise Wi-Fi.
- **Nearby scan:** same SSID/different BSSID and hidden APs remain distinct;
  a Connected mark needs BSSID/radio evidence, not SSID equality. Nearby
  observations never create Saved Devices.
- **Freshness:** FRESH, CACHED, and UNKNOWN are separate. Timestamp evidence
  uses monotonic elapsed time; rejected requests, restrictions, and missing new
  results retain honestly labelled cache rather than reporting an empty scan.
- **Signal:** valid RSSI maps to Excellent at >= -50 dBm, Good at -51..-65,
  Fair at -66..-75, Weak at <= -76. Current/Nearby/Home share the classifier
  where raw RSSI exists. Icons are accompanied by text and dBm semantics.
  Signal grade is not throughput, latency, Internet health, or a network fault.
- **Security:** structured Android values or conservative capability tokens
  distinguish Open, WEP, WPA, WPA2, WPA3, WPA/WPA2, WPA2/WPA3, OWE,
  Enterprise, generic PSK, and Unknown where evidence supports them. Generic
  PSK is not asserted to be WPA2; labels are not security verdicts.
- **Channels:** CH, observed AP count, strongest RSSI, and connected marker
  only. Count is not load, congestion, utilization, or interference. 60 GHz
  may be recognized if returned but is outside the 2.4/5/6 GHz overview.
  6 GHz visibility depends on actual device/platform/environment observations.

observation itself does not upload diagnostic data.
