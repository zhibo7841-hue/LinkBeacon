# LinkBeacon v0.7.0

LinkBeacon v0.7.0 strengthens Device Center with safer saved-device association
and more useful local profile information.

## Highlights

- A smarter and safer Device Center for locally managed devices.
- Choose a Device Type and see a consistent icon for managed devices.
- Add private, local Device Notes and find devices by their localized type or notes.
- Distinguish First Seen, Last Seen, Current address, and Last observed address.
- Keep saved-device data separate when available identity evidence conflicts or
  is not strong enough to prove that two observations are the same device.

## Reliability

- Device association remains network-scoped and evidence-first.
- Conflicting MAC or protocol evidence does not silently transfer Favorites,
  Custom Names, Wake-on-LAN settings, Device Types, or Notes.
- A weak same-network address association may preserve compatible presentation,
  but it does not learn stronger identity evidence or refresh Last Seen.
- Device Type or Notes can independently retain a managed profile.

## Compatibility

- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Existing v0.6.0 installations can upgrade in place without uninstalling or
  clearing app data.
- The additive Room migration preserves saved profile IDs, Favorites, Custom
  Names, Wake-on-LAN configuration, observation metadata, Last Seen, and History.
- Existing profiles do not receive an invented First Seen timestamp during migration.

## Privacy

- Device Type and Notes remain on the device.
- No account or cloud upload is required.
- No ads.
- LinkBeacon remains local-first and does not add background presence monitoring.

## Current Limitations

- First Seen applies to managed profiles created from reliable current
  observations; older profiles may correctly show that it was not recorded.
- A device not observed in the current scan is not automatically classified as offline.
- v0.7.0 does not add multi-network management, background monitoring, or a
  manual Merge/Split workflow.
